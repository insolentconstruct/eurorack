**Beta 3 “Smirr”** — alternative Beads firmware. Standard options default to stock behaviour.

**TL;DR:** the Plaits synth now has banks of eight models, as on Plaits, and random pitch can pick from the last notes sent to PITCH. [Hygrostat](https://hygrostat.insolentconstruct.net/) adds nine optional choices, including a gentler dry/wet, reverb on the grains only, a fade for the delay-mode SEED pop and three more options pages. Stock behaviour stays the default.

Released 2026-11-16 · tag `drizzlet-2026.11.16`

## New since Beta 2

### In the standard firmware

- **Plaits banks:** FEEDBACK selects one of eight models in the current bank, as on Plaits: green (Plaits' first bank, the default), red (noise and drums) or yellow (the Plaits 1.2 models). While Plaits plays, hold [A] and press FREEZE to step to the next bank: [A] shows the bank's colour for half a second, FREEZE stays as it was and the bank is saved. Elsewhere [A]+FREEZE still trims the buffer. This replaces Beta 2's 24 models on one knob.
- **Recent notes:** a third value for Random pitch steps (page 1, DRY/WET). With PITCH patched and its attenurandomizer counter-clockwise, each new grain plays one of the last notes sent to PITCH, transposed by the PITCH knob and following Pitch scale, so a slow melody becomes a moving chord. Just left of noon it plays the newest note; fully counter-clockwise it chooses from the last eight. A note counts once the CV settles; a glided note counts where the glide stops, for glides faster than half a semitone per second. Unpatched, or in the wavetable synth, it plays as Free.
- **Random pitch steps now has three zones:** Free (green), Fifths and octaves (yellow, now the middle third) and Recent notes (red). A saved Fifths and octaves stays selected; to choose it again, turn the knob to the middle.
- **Page 4 LEDs:** options page 4 now pulses FEEDBACK and DRY/WET together (Beta 2: all three).
- **Dimmed quality LED:** at 66% and 33% LED brightness, white is no longer dimmed twice as much as the other colours (Beta 2). The quality LED's red now dims a little faster and its green and blue keep a small lift, a rule chosen by eye on a module; it will be measured with a light sensor in Beta 4. 100% is unchanged.
- **Plaits Speech:** changing the Speech model's word bank no longer overloads the processor for one audio block. The new bank loads over a few milliseconds instead: a word started meanwhile begins 2 to 7 ms later, then sounds exactly as before.

### New Hygrostat choices

Each is off (stock behaviour) unless chosen in [Hygrostat](https://hygrostat.insolentconstruct.net/). None needs a new knob or gesture.

- **Gentler dry/wet:** the grains come in more slowly on DRY/WET. At 9 o'clock they sit 7.6 dB under the dry, against 2.5 dB in stock (Clouds: 8.8 dB). The dry level and both ends of the knob are unchanged.
- **Reverb on the grains only:** only the grains go to the reverb, before the dry/wet mix, as on Clouds. Fully dry has no reverb.
- **Manual gain at power-on:** start in manual gain at unity instead of automatic gain. A saved manual gain still wins, and holding [A] still restarts automatic gain.
- **Attack sensitivity:** Normal, Low (needs a bigger jump in level to count as an attack) or High (catches quieter hits, and more false attacks on noisy input).
- **SEED unlatched at power-on:** SEED presses, gates and, with Snap and trigger, detected attacks start the grains.
- **PITCH without notches:** the knob keeps its intervals in the same places but glides between them, without the flat zones. PITCH CV is unchanged.
- **Delay-mode SEED latch fade:** latching or unlatching SEED in delay mode dips the delay for a moment instead of popping.
- **Options pages 5–7** for custom layouts. Their LEDs: FEEDBACK and REVERB, DRY/WET and REVERB, then all three. Taps skip empty pages; the factory layout stays four pages.
- **Options timeout:** close the options pages after 5, 10, 20 or 30 seconds without a button press, page change or turn of an option knob, keeping your changes.

The gentler dry/wet and grains-only reverb apply to granular, delay and spectral; both synths are unchanged. Turned off, every choice leaves the sound exactly as before.

## Controls

Hold [M] for three seconds, then release, to open the options. Tap [M] to change pages; turn a knob and wait one second to save.

| Page / LED | FEEDBACK | DRY/WET | REVERB |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 / FEEDBACK | Pitch scale | Random pitch steps | Attacks |
| 2 / DRY/WET | Frozen feedback | Delay mode | Reverb character |
| 3 / REVERB | Processor | Synth | Synth start |
| 4 / FEEDBACK and DRY/WET | LED brightness | SEED latch | Limiter |

The [options guide](https://github.com/insolentconstruct/eurorack/blob/drizzlet-2026.11.16/beads/build/docs/OPTIONS.md) explains every option.

## Hygrostat

[Hygrostat](https://hygrostat.insolentconstruct.net/) builds custom firmware and a matching guide in your browser: choose features, controls, defaults and scales. It now remembers your setup in that browser between visits; a shared link still opens the shared setup. Profiles are not uploaded.

## Update and recovery

- Stock settings, Beta 2 settings and calibration carry over. Options, width and diffusion from Beta 1 and earlier reset to their defaults.
- Disconnect speakers during audio updates: the update audio can harm hearing.
- Never use a full-chip or mass erase: it also wipes calibration and settings.
- If all LEDs blink fast for three seconds at power-on, calibration was lost. Power on holding SEED to repair it, then recalibrate.
- Disable monophonic grains before downgrading. Beta 2 plays a saved Recent notes as Free.

The [README](https://github.com/insolentconstruct/eurorack/blob/drizzlet-2026.11.16/beads/build/README.md) covers installation, recovery and downgrading.

## Tested on a module

TL;DR: on one module, a loud granular patch with every new option on peaked at 82% of the audio time budget with no audio underrun, and audio stays clean up to about 91% load at 48 kHz, 87% at 32 kHz and 83% at 24 kHz. The CPU, delay, Plaits, Speech and LED rows below were measured on a timing build of this release's code (the same code plus cycle counters, with a test Hygrostat profile); the clean-load limits and the per-quality rows come from separate test builds that can hold the audio work at a set load.

- **CPU:** loud music, high feedback, heavy granular settings and every new option on peaked at 82.1%, with no audio underrun. In a granular stress test on an earlier test build that peaks at 84–85%, each of six new options alone changed the load by about half a point or less. Recent notes changed it by under a quarter of a point.
- **Headroom:** in a separate test build that held the audio work at a set load, audio stayed clean up to 90.8% at 48 kHz (Bright digital, Sunny tape, synths), 86.6% at 32 kHz (Cold digital) and 82.7% at 24 kHz (Scorched cassette). On this release's code with the hold off, a heavy granular patch peaked at 81.3% in Bright digital (9.5 points under its limit), 63.1% in Sunny tape, 64.6% in Cold digital and 50.9% in Scorched cassette, with no audio underrun.
- **Delay-mode SEED latch fade:** in delay mode with music playing, the fade and the other new mix options on peaked at 62.1%, against 61.8% with them off, with no audio underrun. Whether the fade removes the pop on the module was not settled: without the fade, the test did not pop at every latch change.
- **Plaits banks:** [A]+FREEZE stepped to red, yellow and green, and each bank was saved. Stepping FEEDBACK through each bank's eight models peaked at 70.7% (red), 81.1% (yellow) and 64.4% (green), within the 48 kHz limit, with no audio underrun.
- **Plaits Speech word bank:** stepping HARMONICS (TIME) through the word banks peaked at 43.9% of the time budget, against 31.8% with the bank held, with no audio underrun. An earlier test build without the fix overran one audio block (169.8%).
- **Options timeout:** set to 5 seconds, the pages closed by themselves 5.0 seconds after the last press. Tapping [M] through the pages kept them open; they then closed by themselves.
- **Recent notes:** on an earlier test build with the same Recent notes code, 93.4% of steps of a stepped melody into PITCH played only notes from the last eight (in any octave), and 94.5% with 0.3-second glides.
- **Dimmed quality LED:** the dimming rule was chosen by eye on a module. The release code's LED levels read back as computed at 100%, 66% and 33% brightness, and the white quality LED looked white at 33%.

Still pending on a module: the audio install and the upgrade from Beta 2.

All 20 host test programs pass and all 496 deliberate faults are detected.

## Known issues

- Changing the quality between sample rates still makes a small click, as in Beta 2 ([report](https://github.com/insolentconstruct/eurorack/issues/53)). A full fix is planned for a later release.
- Without the new fade, SEED latch/unlatch can still pop in delay mode, as in the stock firmware ([report](https://github.com/insolentconstruct/eurorack/issues/183)).
- One Beta 1 user's module stopped responding after the audio update ([report](https://github.com/insolentconstruct/eurorack/issues/112)). If an update goes wrong, please comment there.
