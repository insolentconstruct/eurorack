**Beta 1 "Mist", a call for testers.** New features on a three-page options page, all defaulting to stock behaviour, plus a faster grain engine. Settings saved by the alphas are kept. Everything so far was tested on one module; please try it on yours and [report what you find](https://github.com/insolentconstruct/eurorack/issues).

## New features

Most settings are on the **options page**: hold the CV-assign button [M] for three seconds, then release. Tap [M] to change page, turn a mix knob to change its setting; it saves about a second after you stop. Close by holding [M] again or pressing another button. Guides: [printable](https://github.com/insolentconstruct/eurorack/blob/c731eac/beads/build/docs/options-guide.svg) and [phone](https://github.com/insolentconstruct/eurorack/blob/c731eac/beads/build/docs/options-guide-mobile.svg), with every option and the [A] gestures. They are also attached below, with a text version (OPTIONS.md); the copies in the zips are older.

| Page (pulsing LED) | FEEDBACK | DRY/WET | REVERB |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (REVERB) | Pitch scale | Stereo width | Reverb character |
| 2 (FEEDBACK) | Not used yet | Delay mode | Not used yet |
| 3 (DRY/WET) | Wavetable synth | Limiter | Not used yet |

Listed by the number of requests on the [roadmap](https://github.com/insolentconstruct/eurorack/issues/32).

- **Clouds-style reverb** (page 1; 11 requests, #2 and #22): the Clouds reverb's timings and modulation, plus diffusion on the grain output.
- **Buffer trim** (4 requests, #31): hold the quality button [A], then press FREEZE to start a fresh recording window; the next freeze keeps only what was recorded since. Saved buffers keep the trimmed length.
- **Stereo width** (page 1; 4 requests, #5): spreads grains randomly across the stereo field. Can be a fourth [M] destination for CV control.
- **Delay mode** (page 2; 4 requests, #9 and #26): Auto (Stock), Never (SIZE fully clockwise stays granular) or Wide detent (neutral middle of the delay filter, no resonant boost).
- **Pitch scales** (page 1; 4 requests, #6 and #8): Off, Chromatic or Scale; with Scale, tap SEED to step through eight Marbles scales. Each grain's pitch, including randomness, snaps to the scale at its start.
- **Wavetable synth on demand** (page 3; 3 requests, #17): Automatic (Stock) starts it 10 seconds after both inputs are unplugged, as before. Manual never starts it by itself: with both inputs unplugged and FREEZE off, hold [A] for about a second and a half to start or stop it.
- **Monophonic grains** (2 requests, #31): hold [A], then press SEED. Works best with the square and decay envelopes.
- **Output limiter** (page 3): Stock, Fast release or Off.

Limiter and self-play settings saved on alpha 1 or 2 carry over. Self-play is now called Wavetable synth, and On request is now Manual.

## Bug fixes

Compared with the Mutable Instruments source:

- **Flash wear from a modulated FREEZE.** Stock firmware starts a new save each time FREEZE stays on for 10 seconds, so a 20-second LFO on FREEZE means up to about 4,000 erases a day against a 10,000 rating. Drizzlet waits longer after each interrupted save, up to about five minutes, so a modulated FREEZE now writes once instead of every cycle. A completed save, or a minute with FREEZE off, resets the wait.
- **Frozen-buffer saves** carry a checksum and are range-checked on load; an interrupted save is never restored, and changing a setting during a save no longer breaks it. Stock and Drizzlet still load each other's saves.
- **Frozen delay:** negative FM no longer reads outside the buffer, and very fast SEED edges no longer silence the play heads.
- **Delay clock:** no tempo glitch on the first clock after about 12 hours without one.
- Two undefined memory reads fixed, with no audible change.

## Faster, same sound

The app is built with GCC 9 instead of 4.9.3 (about 2% faster), and three small code changes cut the heaviest stock grain case from 97,255 to 90,333 cycles per block (about 6% of the audio budget, measured on a development board). The sound is bit-identical to vanilla in every emulated comparison; see [OPTIMIZATIONS.md](https://github.com/insolentconstruct/eurorack/blob/{TAG}/beads/build/docs/OPTIMIZATIONS.md). On the module a full audio block uses 60% to 90.5% of the budget, depending on settings, with no underruns.

## Coming from the 2021 or 2024 firmware

Drizzlet includes everything Mutable Instruments changed after those releases: settings recovery for modules stuck on boot, smoother knobs and delay controls, and the grain-scheduling change after which our module no longer crackled at high DENSITY. [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/insolentconstruct/eurorack/blob/{TAG}/beads/build/CHANGELOG.md) lists every difference.

## How it was tested

164 host tests on every push (110 of the 117 manual behaviours), reproducible builds, identical emulator audio where nothing was meant to change, and about 13 hours of automated runs on a module compared against vanilla. 48 firmware installs, none failed to boot. Details: [TESTING.md](https://github.com/insolentconstruct/eurorack/blob/{TAG}/beads/build/docs/TESTING.md). Code follows the Mutable Instruments Beads style ([STYLE.md](https://github.com/insolentconstruct/eurorack/blob/{TAG}/beads/build/docs/STYLE.md)).

## Downgrading

- Turn monophonic grains off first.
- Stock firmware loads trimmed saves at full length.
- Saving settings on stock firmware clears the Drizzlet options.

## Known limits

- Tested on one module only. Options page LED colours were checked by eye for all pitch scales, the limiter and a few other settings, but not yet for every value.
- Settings are stored as in Mutable Instruments' firmware: each save is added to one flash sector, and about once every 4,000 saves the full sector is erased and rewritten. If the power goes off during that rewrite, which takes a moment, calibration and settings can reset to defaults; the README shows how to back up calibration. Separately, a changed setting saves about a second after you stop turning, so wait a moment before powering off.
- Where the Beads manual and the firmware disagree: [MANUAL_ERRATA.md](https://github.com/insolentconstruct/eurorack/blob/{TAG}/beads/build/docs/MANUAL_ERRATA.md).
