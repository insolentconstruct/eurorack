**Beta 2 “Fog”** — alternative Beads firmware. Standard options default to stock behaviour.

**TL;DR:** adds a Plaits synth, Spectral processing, attack snapping, evolving frozen
feedback, an editable scale bank and custom firmware from [Hygrostat](https://hygrostat.insolentconstruct.net/).
Tested on a 2021 Beads module: peak processor load below stock on the same heavy patches
(granular 79% against 91%, wavetable synth 84% against 86%), no audio dropouts.

## What's new

- **Plaits synth:** six voices, 24 models (page 3, DRY/WET).
- **Spectral processor** (page 3, FEEDBACK). Switching processor clears the recording.
- **Attacks:** grains start on recorded hits, optionally with triggers (page 1, REVERB).
- **Evolving frozen feedback** (page 2, FEEDBACK).
- **Random fifths and octaves** for PITCH randomness (page 1, DRY/WET).
- **Fourth options page:** LED brightness, SEED latch, limiter.
- **Grain diffusion:** hold [A] and turn REVERB. **Stereo width** moves to [A]+DRY/WET.
- **Scale bank:** edit, reorder or import up to 14 scales in Hygrostat.
- Synth reverb tails are kept, as in stock; Hygrostat can clear them instead.

Hold [M] for three seconds, then release, to open the options. Tap [M] to change pages;
turn a knob and wait one second to save.

| 1 / FEEDBACK | Pitch scale | Random pitch steps | Attacks |
| 2 / DRY/WET | Frozen feedback | Delay mode | Reverb character |
| 3 / REVERB | Processor | Synth | Synth start |
| 4 / all three | LED brightness | SEED latch | Limiter |

## Hygrostat

[Hygrostat](https://hygrostat.insolentconstruct.net/) builds custom firmware and a matching guide in
your browser: choose features, controls, defaults and scales. Its processor-load figures are estimates;
it warns about, or refuses, combinations that may be too heavy.

## Update and recovery

- Stock settings and calibration carry over. Upgrading from an earlier Drizzlet resets
  Drizzlet options, width and diffusion to their defaults.
- Disconnect speakers during audio updates: the update audio can harm hearing.
- Never use a full-chip or mass erase: it also wipes calibration and settings.
- If all LEDs blink fast for three seconds at power-on, calibration was lost. Power on
  holding SEED to repair it, then recalibrate.
- Disable monophonic grains before downgrading.

The [README](https://github.com/insolentconstruct/eurorack/blob/{TAG}/beads/build/README.md)
covers installation, recovery and downgrading.

## Tested on a module

Peak processor load of the audio callback, measured on a 2021 Beads module with timing builds
(Stock is a build of the vanilla source; Beta 2 with standard options):

| Heavy test patch | Stock | Tuneup | Beta 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Granular | 91% | 77% | 79% |
| Wavetable synth | 86% | 83% | 84% |
| Wavetable synth, Clouds-style reverb, SIZE 97% | | | 84% |
| Plaits string model | | | 70% |
| Spectral | | | 18% |

No audio dropouts or late spectral frames.

- Installing by audio and storage repair worked.
- After an upgrade from Beta 1, or with an invalid Hygrostat profile, the module started
  with the expected settings and kept its calibration.
- Settings survived a power cycle, and starting Beta 1 or a vanilla build once
  (without saving) before returning to Beta 2.
